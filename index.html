🎮 Camera & Battlefield Controls

  • Select & Command: Click or tap any of your living stone warriors to illuminate legal destination runes (emerald/cyan rings for moves, crimson runes for captures), then click the target tile.
  • Orbit & Zoom Camera: Left-drag on empty space or Right-drag anywhere to orbit around the Gothic chamber. Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom.
  • Shortcuts: Press C to cycle camera views, F to flip perspective, Z to undo, or Space to skip an active combat cutscene.
