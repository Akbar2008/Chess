# The Enchanted Chess — Cinematic 3D Wizard Chess

A complete, playable 3D fantasy stone-warrior chess experience inspired by the giant medieval castle chamber.

## Features
- **Monumental 8x8 Marble & Obsidian Chessboard** inside a moonlit Gothic castle chamber with flickering braziers, volumetric moonbeams, and surrounding shattered warrior statues.
- **32 Articulated Living Stone Warriors**: Pawn (Stone Soldier), Knight (Armored Horse Rider), Bishop (Magical Guardian), Rook (Fortress Guardian), Queen (Supreme Magical Warrior), and King (Royal Stone Guardian).
- **6-Phase Cinematic Combat & Stone Destruction System**: Recognition, Approach, Director Camera Tracking, Class-Specific Weapon/Magic Strike, Physics Stone Shattering, and Smooth Return.
- **Full Chess Rules & Minimax AI**: Check, Checkmate, Stalemate, Castling, En Passant, Promotion, Threefold Repetition, 50-Move Rule, Move History, Undo, and Easy/Medium/Hard AI.
- **Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer**: Subterranean castle ambience, echoing stone footsteps, sword/lance/warhammer impacts, arcane beams, and stone shattering.
